# Tone Guide

## Default Chinese

Use natural, confident, grounded language. Prefer:

- "我过去主要在..."
- "和这个岗位最相关的有两点..."
- "一个能说明这个能力的经历是..."
- "如果后面有机会，我也想展开..."

Avoid:

- "本人具备优秀的综合素质"
- "我拥有极强的学习能力和沟通能力"
- "贵司平台广阔、发展前景好"
- Resume-style timelines that start with school and list every job.
- A script that sounds like an online template rather than the user's real background.

## Anti-Template Rewrite

A good intro may use a structure, but should not sound templated.

Replace generic claims with concrete behavior:

- `学习能力强` -> "之前在...里，我从不会...到能独立完成..."
- `沟通能力强` -> "我经常需要和...确认信息，把...整理清楚后推进..."
- `责任心强` -> "我负责过...，其中最怕出错的是...，所以我会..."
- `抗压能力强` -> "在...时间紧/信息乱的情况下，我先..."

Use 2-3 short spoken sentences instead of one long formal paragraph. Remove slogans, grand conclusions, and decorative adjectives.

## Version Styles

- Stable formal: calm, concise, suitable for HR and traditional companies; no slang.
- Natural spoken: shorter clauses, less resume language, sounds like something the user can say aloud.
- Anti-AI: remove list-like phrasing, repeated "具备/负责/推动", and claims unsupported by detail.
- Relaxed but professional: warmer opening and smoother transitions, but still evidence-based.
- Xiaohongshu comment: brief, concrete, friendly, and clear about what is assumed or missing.

## English

Rewrite in natural English instead of translating sentence by sentence. Keep sentences short. Use "I have been working on..." and "A relevant example is..." rather than formal self-branding. For English interviews, fewer claims plus one concrete example is better than a dense list of strengths.

## Bilingual

Do not mirror every sentence. Provide a Chinese strategy plus a natural English spoken version, or two compact versions with the same evidence.

## Candidate Types

- Student or junior: emphasize education, internships, projects, learning speed, basic execution, and motivation; avoid pretending senior ownership.
- Fresh graduate without internship: mine coursework, graduation projects, clubs, competitions, part-time work, and tool practice.
- Career changer: lead with transferable evidence, explain the switch briefly, and keep gaps in follow-up notes.
- Junior with ordinary evidence: show reliability, clarity, execution, and one concrete example; do not over-package.
- Senior candidate: lead with career through-line, representative outcomes, methods, and role value.
- Manager: include team size, business results, operating mechanisms, and leadership style only when supported.
- Technical candidate: keep technical terms tied to decisions, tradeoffs, and production outcomes.
- Sales/operation: emphasize target awareness, customer/user understanding, follow-up, execution closure, and data or feedback loops.
- Admin/finance/HR: emphasize accuracy, process, coordination, confidentiality, detail, and low-risk execution.
