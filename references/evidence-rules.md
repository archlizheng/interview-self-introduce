# Evidence Rules

## Evidence Map

Use this table before drafting. If the JD is missing, label the first column as role expectations or common interviewer concerns.

| JD 要求/岗位常见期待 | 重要性 | 用户证据 | 证据强度 | 相关度 | 可讲性 | 风险 | 是否进入自我介绍 |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Score each row:

- Importance: `1-5`; `5` means a core responsibility, hard requirement, or likely first-round concern.
- Evidence strength: `0-5`; `5` means clear task, action, result, and supportable detail.
- Relevance: `1-5`; same domain or same capability gets higher scores.
- Speakability: `1-5`; can be explained safely in limited time.
- Risk: `0-5`; high if the claim is vague, exaggerated, unsupported, or likely to be challenged.

Formula:

```text
matchScore = 重要性 * 0.35
           + 证据强度 * 0.30
           + 相关度 * 0.20
           + 可讲性 * 0.10
           - 风险 * 0.05
```

Choose the top 2-3 low-risk, high-relevance themes for the main intro.

## Mining Ordinary Experience

When the user says they have "no content" or provides thin evidence, look for small but speakable proof:

- Course projects, graduation projects, competitions, clubs, volunteer work, part-time work, internships.
- Spreadsheet work, document writing, customer communication, user research, sales follow-up, activity execution, process coordination.
- Moments where the user handled ambiguity, found an error, organized messy information, improved a small process, learned a tool, or got feedback from a teacher, manager, customer, or teammate.

Convert each ordinary experience into:

```text
task -> action -> difficulty -> result -> reflection -> role relevance
```

Use conservative wording when results are not quantified:

- "参与整理..."
- "协助核对..."
- "负责其中的..."
- "把零散信息整理成..."
- "在这个过程中练到的是..."

Weak evidence can enter the intro only as a modest proof point. Do not inflate it into ownership, seniority, or business impact.

## Truthfulness

- Every capability claim must point to resume evidence, project notes, interview feedback, or user-provided facts.
- If the JD is missing, do not claim company-specific fit; say "基于这个岗位的常见要求，我先假设...".
- Do not invent metrics, company scale, role titles, team size, revenue, user count, technical ownership, or interview outcomes.
- Mark uncertain facts as `需确认` or phrase them conservatively.
- Weak evidence belongs in the content-mining notes, prep notes, or possible follow-up strategy unless it is directly relevant and safely phrased.

## Resume Compression

Do not retell the resume chronologically. This does not mean ignoring the resume. Compress it into 2-3 role-relevant proof points:

- What identity should the interviewer remember?
- Which 2-3 experiences best prove role fit?
- What should the interviewer naturally ask next?

Avoid long school-to-job timelines unless the user explicitly needs a life-history version.

## Risky Wording

Treat these as high-risk unless directly supported nearby:

`唯一负责` `全面主导` `显著提升` `大幅增长` `行业领先` `从0到1全权` `完全负责` `核心负责人` `精通` `专家级` `通过率100%` `保证拿offer`

Safer replacements:

- `唯一负责` -> `主要负责其中的...`
- `全面主导` -> `负责推进...并与...协作`
- `显著提升` -> `在输入材料中的指标范围内说明提升`
- `精通` -> `熟悉并在...项目中使用`
- `保证拿offer` -> remove it; never promise outcomes

## Duration

- Chinese: about 220-280 characters per minute.
- English: about 130-160 words per minute.
- A 30s Chinese intro should usually contain 110-140 Chinese characters.
- A 60s Chinese intro should usually contain 180-240 Chinese characters.
- A 90s Chinese intro should usually contain 280-380 Chinese characters.
- A 120s Chinese intro should usually contain 380-480 Chinese characters.
