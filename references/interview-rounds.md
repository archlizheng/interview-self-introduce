# Interview Rounds

Adjust the same evidence for the round.

| Round | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| HR | Clear identity, stable motivation, communication, low risk, compensation or timeline only if asked |
| Hiring manager | Role understanding, representative results, business judgment, collaboration, ownership boundary |
| Technical | Stack, implementation detail, tradeoffs, debugging, reliability, measurable engineering details |
| Final | Career through-line, long-term motivation, organizational contribution, seniority fit |
| English | Direct structure, shorter sentences, no Chinese-style long clauses |

## Round Defaults

- HR: avoid dense project detail; prepare risk explanations separately.
- Hiring manager: pick the most JD-relevant project and explain why it transfers.
- Technical: include one concrete implementation detail and one tradeoff.
- Final: connect the role to the candidate's next 2-3 year direction.
- English: use fewer claims and stronger examples.

## Candidate Scenario Emphasis

| Scenario | Lead With | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| `freshGraduateNoInternship` | Education, course/project evidence, learning speed, motivation | Pretending to have mature workplace ownership |
| `freshGraduateWithInternship` | Internship task, collaboration, delivery, reflection | Listing every school activity |
| `careerChanger` | Transferable skills, switch logic, proof of preparation | Apologizing for gaps for too long |
| `juniorNoHighlight` | Reliable execution, concrete small wins, role relevance | Inflated adjectives and unsupported confidence |
| `seniorProfessional` | Career through-line, representative outcomes, decision quality | Overloading the opening with every project |
| `englishInterview` | Short natural English, one relevant example, clear motivation | Literal translation from Chinese |
| `salesOrOperation` | Customer/user understanding, follow-up, goals, execution loop | Empty "people skills" claims |
| `adminFinanceHR` | Accuracy, process, confidentiality, coordination, detail | Overstating strategy or leadership |
| `technicalOrProduct` | Problem, decision, tradeoff, shipped outcome, learning | Tool-name lists without context |

## Quick-Start Assumptions

If the user gives only role and partial background, state assumptions after the scripts:

- "基于这个岗位的常见要求，我先假设面试官会关注..."
- "这一版没有使用具体公司 JD，所以没有写公司特定匹配。"
- "如果你补充 JD/项目细节，我会把通用表达换成更准的证据。"
